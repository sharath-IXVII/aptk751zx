# ENT true-crime Shorts: house rules (from the owner)

- 2 Shorts a day (12:47, 19:43 IST), YouTube + Instagram. Quality over volume (owner, Nov 2026).
- Real historical cases only; everyone involved long dead. No gore, no sexual content shown or described.
- Story must be very clear: never skip a step; a part may run up to 90 s.
- Every part continues exactly where the previous one ended (no "Previously" recaps).
- More detail: ~10-12 scenes per part, characters acting it out, speaker name above every caption.
- Every part ends with a viewer question. Title card "BASED ON A REAL STORY" first.
